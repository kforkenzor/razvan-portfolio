Screenshots for the Magitale case study.

Captured 2026-09-18 from the live marketing site (https://magitale.com/) with
headless Chrome at 1600px wide, then cropped per section.

  01-home.png    Hero — headline, App Store and Google Play badges, and the app
                 home screen in a phone mockup. Captured at 2x and downscaled,
                 cropped above the cookie banner.
  02-wizard.png  "Create Stories As Unique As Your Child" — the four-step story
                 wizard, showing three real app screens (Pick a Lesson, Choose a

  03-guides.png  "Meet the Guides" — the four narrator personas (Luna, Max, Lily,

These are the client's own marketing visuals, not raw device captures. 01 and 02
are the ones that show it is a native mobile app; keep 01 as images[0].
